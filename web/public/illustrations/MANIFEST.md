## Color principle (settled)

Standing principle for all Storyset downloads, current and future — see
`raw/README.md`'s Human notes for full wording:

- **Teal** — doctor/hospital/clinical-system-facing.
- **Coral** — patient shown *doing* something (agency/empowerment) — coral
  as the dominant fill is correct here, not an accent-only color.
- **Navy** — patient shown *experiencing* something passively/difficult,
  used sparingly.

## Attribution

**One combined credit line covers the whole product — no need to name each
category/collection separately.** Freepik's (Storyset's parent) official
attribution requirement is a single fixed line — "Designed by Freepik" (or
the illustration-specific author name, where given) with a link back to
freepik.com or storyset.com — placed once, either next to the resource or
in the site's footer/credits section. The requirement is about the *credit
being present and visible*, not about enumerating every collection a
resource came from; the per-illustration "collection attribution" links in
the table above (Work, Event, People, etc.) are Storyset's suggested
per-page link, not a mandate to list all of them. A single footer/credits
line naming Storyset with a link to https://storyset.com is sufficient for
this whole app.
Source: Freepik's attribution documentation (support.freepik.com /
freepik.com "Attribution: How, when and where") — the fixed "Designed by
Freepik" credit line format, placed near the asset or in the footer,
independent of how many resources are used.
